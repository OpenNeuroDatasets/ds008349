# A comprehensive physiological dataset of pain and aversive modalities for pain biomarkers research 

This dataset contains EEG recordings from 125 pain-free adults, as well as galvanic skin response and electrocardiogram data in a subset of 76 participants, from two resting-state and four experimental conditions (thermal and chemical pain; aversive auditory and taste stimulations). Behavioural, sociodemographic, and psychological data are also available in the participants.tsv file. 

This work was funded by the *Fonds de Recherche du Québec - Santé (FRQS)*, the *Fonds de Recherche du Québec - Nature et technologie (FRQNT)*, the *Réseau de bio-imagerie du Québec (RBIQ)*, the *Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada (CRSNG)*, and the *Canadian Pain Society (CPS)*.
